Continue-As-New

A subscription bills every cycle until its Event History grows too long and the Temporal Service ends it. Add Continue-As-New and let it run for as long as the customer stays.

  1. Watch a long-running subscription's Event History grow.
  2. Add Continue-As-New and carry its state into the next run.
  3. Watch the chain of runs in the Temporal UI.
